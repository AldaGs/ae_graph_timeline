After Effects will not tell us when the user edits the comp we generated. Can we notice — cheaply enough to check on every diff, and precisely enough to say what moved? Snapshots are keyed by layer id, never index: an index-keyed snapshot calls a one-layer reorder "everything changed", which is true and useless.
